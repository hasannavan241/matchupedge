How it works
Our call
Every game gets a call on who wins, mixed from two views. The betting market: every book’s price with its cut removed, and the middle value across books, so one stale book can’t move it. Our team view: power ratings from this season and last, every factor at full strength, and news researched each morning (injuries and returns, trades and signings, coaching changes, drama, motivation), each scored in points or goals for a team, with sources. Each league uses the mix that picked the most winners in testing, with ratings and factors built only from earlier games: NFL 85% market and 15% team view (66.7% of winners over 20 seasons, against 66.5% for the betting favorite; 67.3% against 66.7% over the last six), NBA 90% and 10% (68.5% over 18 seasons, level with the favorite), and in the Premier League the market alone once lines post (no mix did better over 14 seasons; before lines post, the call is the team view). The researched news can’t be backtested; the model record under My bets tracks it from here.
Best bets
Best bets come from the tested model: the market plus only the factors that held up when tested on years of games, each at the share the closing line missed. NFL: wind and cold 50%, a backup quarterback 45% (spread and moneyline), Denver altitude 10%. NBA: altitude 50%, players out 20%, long trips 15%, back-to-backs 15%. Premier League: about 9% of a shots-on-target rating’s gap on goal difference (10% on the total), −0.011 goals per point of last-5 form difference, and +0.18 goals for a team playing its second league match in 3 days. Pace, head to head, rest, referees, Elo and power ratings count 0%. NFL weights can be changed under Tested model settings. You can switch the best bets to our call’s own pricing, but those are marked untested: see What testing found.
Best bet and value
Every price at your books is valued against our call (or the tested fair line): value = chance to win × profit − chance to lose. NFL margins use a 13.5-point curve with extra weight on key numbers like 3 and 7; the NBA plain curves (12 points for spreads, 18 for totals); soccer a goal model fitted to each match’s home, draw and away prices and totals, where quarter lines count as two half bets. Whole-number lines can push. Strong is +5% or more, Lean +2% to +5%, Thin 0 to +2%. No edge means even the best price is below fair value after the book’s cut: the least-bad way to bet that game, not a bet with an advantage.
Stakes
With a bankroll set under My bets, each positive-value pick gets a share of the Kelly bet for its value (¼ by default), capped at a share of the bankroll (2% by default). Full Kelly on claimed edges wiped out the bankroll in testing. Without a bankroll, positive-value picks show 1 unit.
Tracking
Log a bet from any pick or with the form under My bets; it grades itself from the final score, with its closing-line value: its value against the fair closing line (NFL closes from nflverse; NBA and Premier League from DraftKings via ESPN). Beating the close over many bets is the quickest sign an edge is real. Bets save privately to your account. The model record keeps our calls and both styles’ best bets the same way.
What testing found

Our call’s own bets, last six seasons (2020-21 to 2025-26, 1 unit per bet at historical closing prices, news not included): 6,696 bets lost 532.6 units, −8.0% per bet. NFL −13.3% on 1,026 bets, NBA −6.9% on 3,624, Premier League −7.2% on 2,046 (−3.5% at the best price across books). Every league lost in every season. The tested model on the same games: −2.6% on 2,688 bets (NFL +1.5%, NBA −2.3%, Premier League −4.0%). Picking winners and beating prices are different jobs: the market already prices who is likely to win.

NFL, 5,246 games. Projecting scores from scratch lost 7.9% per bet against closing lines since 2016. Only wind held up: unders in 15+ mph wind went 240–194, +7.6% per bet. NFL backtest

NBA, 22,810 games. Full projections lost 5.3% per bet at the close. When a rotation player was newly ruled out, the spread moved toward his opponent 71% of the time, about 1.4 points; opening lines miss about 60% of that, so being early is the edge. Totals 6+ points off the opener moved our way 60% of the time but only broke even after the juice. NBA backtest

Premier League, 5,300 matches (2012–2026). Three small signals held up in every window, but betting them made +2.5% per bet at the best price only because of the two empty-stadium seasons; the other eight lost 2.6%. Treat them as leans.
